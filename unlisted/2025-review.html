2025 in review

Published On: December 29, 2025

Dear Family, Friends and Robots

What is a year made up of? Hours and days and months of course but also ice cream and celebrations and losses and lessons. Let’s chat about all of the things we experienced in 2025.

Let’s start with the kids and their school. Alex is on his last year of school for his degree in Environmental Science. He should be completed all his classes by May 2026. He’s already starting to apply for full time jobs in his field next summer. He was able to land a research grant with the UofL in the spring and summer. He spent much of that time cataloging bryophytes in the Crowsnest Pass, Waterton area and Cypress hills. He continued that work into the fall completing a independent study and paper on what they discovered.

Georgia finished her first year of College with a 4.0 GPA. She has applied for a spot at the UofA and has been accepted to at least one program there. She is awaiting the final decision on the program she really wants which is Laboratory Science. She took part time schooling at the college in the fall as she is running out of courses that can transfer. Instead she spent more time working at the water testing lab where she is the lab division leader. She’s doing quite well, but is very excited to move to Edmonton in summer 2026.

We ended up having two brushes with fire departments this year. One was for a suspected gas leak in our house. Turns out is was just some sewer backup issues that we now have resolved. We went to Edmonton with Georgia in May to explore the University a bit and to just have a quick trip. One evening something went wrong in our hotel and we all had to evacuate while the Edmonton fire department checked things out. It didn’t take long and we were back inside for the night.

In June I got to see my cousins from Quebec I hadn’t seen in years. Mylene and Caroline and their friends came to visit Alberta. They stopped in Beaver Mines where my parents live for an extended weekend. I took them through Waterton and we did a couple hikes. It was great catching up with them, I hope to visit them in Quebec again soon.

The summer brought a couple of significant milestones in 2025. Joleen and I celebrated our 25th wedding anniversary and I celebrated my 50th birthday. Turning 50 was somehow more joyus than turning 40 was. I’m happy to be 50 and to be able to see retirement ahead. I’m excited for what the next 10 years will bring especially watching the kids fully embrace adulthood. As a happy 50th birthday gift, I bought myself a car I’ve been wanting for a while, an Ioniq 6. It still brings me a lot of joy every time I drive it. Sadly I had to say goodbye to my old Nissan Frontier, it was a great truck.

Joleen and I took a vacation to Niagra Falls region in August to celebrate our milestones. The Jackson Triggs winery there has a beautiful outdoor concert venue. We have been watching the schedule for a number of years in hopes we could take one of those concerts in. We were able to see Donovan Woods, one of our favourite Canadian singer/songwriters there. The concert and venue was so great and made the trip worth it.

After we cam back from vacation I took a quick trip to Lethbridge where Alex and I recorded a special radio show together for my birthday! We used all my vinyl to put together a best of show from my collection. It was a ton of fun, you can listen to it here -> Aug 22nd CKXU Radio Show

Leo (the dog) is 12 years old now. He’s still doing well but he has slowed down a lot this year. He had some serious dental issues this fall and had to have 6 teeth removed. He has since recovered very well though and we treasure every minute we get with him. He still get’s 2.5 km of walks in every day. Speaking of dogs, Joleen’s parents said a final goodbye to their 14 year old dog Chance in the spring. They were quite sad about it, he was a significant figure in their life. They did however welcome a new dog, Noble the Papillon, to their family this summer. He is quite a busy but joyful creature to be around.

The fall this year was very beautiful and stable with little wildfire smoke. It was the best fall I can remember in the last 10 years weather wise. Not to let a good fall go to waste I wanted to get one more hike in this year. I was finally able to get Alex to go with me to Crypt Lake in Waterton. This was my second time on this hike and my first with Alex. That is just such a wonderful trail. A truly special experience. I’m so glad Alex and I were able to experience together on such a nice late September day.

Overall 2025 was a good year with good health and we are looking forward to an even better 2026. Next year is full of more significant milestones with Alex graduating and Georgia moving to Edmonton. It will be the beginning of our empty nester phase as well. We are all excited to see how that turns out. On a final note, I’ve begun songwriting again after a long hiatus. I’m hoping to share the new songs with people again soon.

Until next year, be well.
